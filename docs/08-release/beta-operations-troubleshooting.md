# When beta numbers look stale or wrong

Start with the account, company, date range, report name, displayed warning and last successful update. Ask for a screenshot with those fields; never request WB tokens or service credentials. Record the release SHA and incident time.

1. Confirm the selected scope and session membership. Compare the same account and exact period across Dashboard, Product Analytics and the downloaded report. A denied foreign account is an access boundary, not missing data. Refresh the session after a membership change.
2. Read the freshness warning and Sales readiness. Missing/unresolved Sales prices must remain unavailable, not silently zero. Overall last-sync time does not prove Finance, Ads and stock have identical coverage. A missing Product Cost is deliberately 0 and must be described as unresolved.
3. Inspect GitHub Sync Worker runs at the deployed SHA. Check task/account inputs, exit status, budget, summary and whether `SYNC_WORKER_SCHEDULE_ENABLED` is enabled. A missing schedule flag deliberately holds scheduled execution. Check no local timer/manual recovery is competing. Do not dispatch an all-account retry.
4. Read `sync_runs`, `commercial_entity_sync_state`, `commercial_sync_ticks` and the account's last-successful timestamps. Distinguish last attempt from last success. A failed attempt is not a freshness advance. Capture sanitized correlation/run IDs, entity and failure class, not API payloads or credential-bearing headers.
5. Read `finance_incremental_sync_state`: phase/current period, durable cursor, completed ranges, last successful data date, next permitted request time, lease owner/expiry and last error. `rate_limit` means wait until the recorded eligibility time. `lease_busy` means another owner may be active; `lease_lost` means this worker must not commit. A failed page must not advance the durable cursor or complete a week. Never clear locks or reset cursors to make a status green.
6. Verify routing: Account 1 allowlist is `1`, live V1 target true; Account 2 normal incremental target uses recovery campaign false. Historical recovery completion is not current freshness. If a new operator recovery campaign exists, stop and resolve quota ownership before enabling ingestion.
7. Reconcile persisted Sales identities (`marketplace_account_id,sale_id`) and Finance source keys per account. Check SALE and RETURN coexistence, missing prices, date coverage and late corrections. Do not repair source facts with manual aggregate overrides or replay historical migrations.
8. Apply the approved financial contract: Revenue is Finance for_pay; Marketplace Fees are the explicit Finance fee/service total and are informational beside V4 profit; Sales minus Sales forPay is the separate Sales-to-Settlement Difference. Product Cost uses only that product's cost history. Category Settlement remains Legacy. An accounting-rule change is Human Gate 3.
9. Ads and stock need independent coverage checks. Hourly worker defaults do not include Ads. Stock reads remain legacy until a complete canonical reconciliation and explicit cutover. Historical inventory reads remain DB-only; missing snapshots do not justify live WB fallback.
10. If an implementation regression remains, reproduce against the local restored copy and fixture suite. Hold new scheduled jobs, preserve durable state and record a safe checkpoint. A production repair, credential/config change or bounded retry requires the production window. Restore/delete requires the destructive-action gate; do not undo containment policies or rotate the encryption key as troubleshooting.

Logs are evidence only when sanitized. Do not export raw Auth cookies, invitation hashes, marketplace ciphertext or secret values. Revoking refresh sessions does not instantly invalidate every issued JWT; follow the access-removal procedure and token-expiry boundary in the beta checklist.
