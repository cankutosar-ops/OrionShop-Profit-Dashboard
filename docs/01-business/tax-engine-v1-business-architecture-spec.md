# Tax Engine V1 — business and architecture specification

Status: Sprint 1 design only, 24 September 2026. No application behavior, production data, Financial Engine V4 formula, or Product Cost semantics change in this document. Tax amounts described below are estimates, not filed returns or payment instructions. Any source marked `REVIEW` is excluded from a confirmed deduction until its evidence and legal classification are resolved.

## 1. Scope and rules of calculation

The tax subject is one Russian company/legal taxpayer, not an individual WB account. The supported objects are `USN_INCOME` (general rate 6%) and `USN_INCOME_MINUS_EXPENSES` (general rate 15%). `CUSTOM` is a configured rate **and one of those two objects**, not a third tax regime. Store the rate applicable to each effective interval; never infer a regional concession from the seller's address. In 2026 regional reduced rates depend on activity and other statutory conditions, so the user/accountant must supply and attest them. USN income and eligible expenses are cumulative from 1 January; advance calculations use Q1, half-year, and nine-month YTD. The annual 1% minimum applies only to the income-minus-expenses object and is compared with ordinary tax at year end. A 1% reference may appear midyear but must never be called a current amount due. [FNS USN rules](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn77/taxation/taxes/usn/), [FNS 2026 changes](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/new2026/).

Taxable marketplace proceeds are the **full qualifying customer consideration**, before WB withholds commission or services, and not the net bank transfer. For the income-minus-expenses object, agent commission may be an expense; for income-only it does not reduce the base. Refunds reduce income in the period legally recognized for the refund, not necessarily the original sale period. WB-funded discount reimbursements may be separate income when received. VAT, if applicable, must be separated from the USN income base and has its own tax engine; V1 must not silently assume every company is VAT-exempt. [FNS marketplace gross-income example](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn77/news/tax_doc_news/15047439/), [FNS marketplace timing/returns](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn03/news/activities_fts/16317578/), [FNS 2026 VAT/USN changes](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/new2026/). The timing article is archived guidance; have the accountant approve the operational recognition policy against current documents before activating a number.

For income-minus-expenses, expense eligibility requires business purpose, appropriate documentary support, and the applicable payment/recognition condition. From 2026, Tax Code article 346.16(1)(45) also admits certain Chapter 25 expenses; older blanket "closed list only" categorizations would be wrong. Merchandise bought for resale is recognized as expense as paid and sold, with returns/credit adjustments tracked. This is **not** simply invoice date times quantity sold, and insurance-contribution exceptions require their own rules. [FNS 2026 expense expansion](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/html/sites/www.rn59.nalog.ru/Zakoni/202602izmZ.pdf), [FNS purchased goods](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn40/news/tax_doc_news/4614678/), [FNS insurance contribution treatment](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn92/news/tax_doc_news/15408629/).

This initial formula does not include an automatic insurance-contribution tax reduction for income-only companies, past-year loss carryforward, VAT, foreign tax, or statutory return preparation. Those can materially affect actual payable tax; show their exclusion explicitly. Company legal form/employee status and a verified contribution ledger would be needed before implementing the reduction. [FNS contribution rules](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn25/ifns/r25_14/info/16613357/).

## 2. Existing repository facts

- `companies.default_tax_percent` is a single numeric UI input defaulting to 6, with no tax object, effective date, regional proof, VAT status, or legal-form metadata. New company creation currently asks for a percentage rather than a tax model (`20260731200000_company_workspace_11_2.sql`, `company-list-panel.tsx`, `marketplace-account-service.ts`). It must not be read as evidence that an existing company elected USN income.
- Financial Engine V4 historical reporting uses `wb_sales.revenue`, named for Sales API `finishedPrice`, for estimated tax. Smart Pricing deliberately uses a different simulated base. V4 commercial Revenue is signed `wb_finance` `for_pay`/`ppvz_for_pay`; Net Sales uses Sales API `priceWithDisc`; Marketplace Fees use explicit Finance fee/service suffixes, while `Net Sales − Sales API forPay` is the Sales-to-Settlement Difference. V1 does not rewrite these.
- `mapApiSaleToDb` persists absolute `finishedPrice` **or falls back to `forPay`** when `finishedPrice` is absent; it persists `priceWithDisc`, `forPay`, `SALE/RETURN`, and `sale_date`. Returns are positive magnitudes plus a return flag. Thus `wb_sales.revenue` cannot be treated as proven customer-paid amount for every historical row (`wildberries/mappers.ts`).
- WB officially calls its Sales API preliminary, limits guaranteed history to 90 days, warns `finishedPrice`/`priceWithDisc`/`forPay` can be temporary zero for 24 hours, and says the simplified values can differ from realization-report details. Delayed-payment orders may be absent. [WB Sales API](https://dev.wildberries.ru/en/openapi/reports).
- Finance V1 detailed reports provide `retailAmount`, `retailPrice`, `forPay`, `saleDt`, `rrDate`, `reportId`, and `rrdId`; list reports expose `retailAmountSum`, `forPaySum`, period and publication date. Current `WbApiFinanceRow` types contain `retail_amount`, but `mapFinanceRowsFromReport` emits only `for_pay` and fee/adjustment lines: gross retail amount, customer payment timestamp, and separate discount reimbursements are **not** durably represented as tax income facts. Report period and publication date do not themselves prove receipt date. [WB Finance V1 API](https://dev.wildberries.ru/docs/openapi/financial-reports-and-accounting); repository `wildberries/finance-v1.ts`, `wildberries/mappers.ts`.
- `purchases` and `purchase_lines` form an account-scoped supplier invoice/import ledger: date, supplier, optional invoice number, currency and optional exchange rate, product/quantity/unit cost. Import writes lines and appends `product_cost_history` at purchase date, potentially with row-level failures. The table comments say it is **not inventory or ERP**. It records neither supplier payment nor sale-linked cost allocation, credit notes, proof documents, landed-cost/FX tax treatment, or ownership transfer. The cost resolver chooses latest cost by product and can share by supplier article; that is a profitability input, not a tax purchase lot. (`20260628120000_purchase_records.sql`, `20260629120000_purchase_ux_revision.sql`, `20260731120000_purchase_invoice_number.sql`, `purchase-service.ts`, `cost-history-resolution.ts`.)
- Ads are separate `wb_ads.spend` facts; Finance categories include commission, acquiring, PPVZ reward/VW, logistics, return logistics, storage, penalty, adjustment, compensation and other. Finance `acceptance` currently maps to `OTHER`. Some Finance amounts are converted to absolute magnitudes by the P&L mapper. Those P&L facts cannot establish the direction of tax corrections (`finance-category.ts`, `wildberries/mappers.ts`, `wb_ads` schema).

## 3. Taxable income source decision

`TAXABLE_INCOME_SOURCE` is **WB's finalized, gross buyer-payment/agent-receipt facts, including signed refunds and separately classified WB-funded reimbursements, reconciled to published realization reports and payment evidence**. This is the semantic source, not `forPay`, bank payout, or a direct copy of any present V4 field. The exact WB field/timestamp that proves this source is **HUMAN ACCOUNTING DECISION REQUIRED**, because the current persisted facts do not prove cash receipt or all discounts. Do not activate a numeric tax estimate until source reconciliation and sign/date policy are approved.

Candidate evaluation:

- `priceWithDisc` (`wb_sales.price_with_disc`): seller-discounted merchandise display value. It may exclude WB-funded discounts, is preliminary/late, and dates sale activity rather than proven cash receipt. Useful reconciliation candidate, not tax authority.
- `finishedPrice` (`wb_sales.revenue`): nearest existing customer-paid proxy; signed using `SALE/RETURN`, but missing can fall back to seller `forPay`, zeros can be temporary, some payment modes absent, and refund timing unproved. Keep as V4 estimate; cannot certify tax base without per-row provenance.
- Sales API `forPay` (`wb_sales.for_pay`): seller-payable goods settlement, after marketplace economics. Exclude as taxable gross base.
- Finance `forPay`/`ppvz_for_pay` (`wb_finance` `for_pay` suffix): finalized seller-payable settlement, not gross customer consideration. Exclude as taxable gross base.
- Finance V1 `retailAmount`/`retailAmountSum`: gross realization candidate from published reports, but the API schema alone does not establish equivalence to cash received, payout/refund timing, WB discount reimbursement, or VAT netting. Current mapper does not persist it. Use in a bounded, sanitized field-level reconciliation against WB financial documents and bank/receipt evidence before choosing it as a tax event amount.

Sprint 2 must prove: account-scoped report completeness and unique `reportId/rrdId`; gross amount matched to seller's issued/agent report for sale, return, partial refund, WB-funded discount, delayed payment and report correction; payment/credit effective date; VAT handling; old Sales API `finishedPrice` versus new source differences. Persist gross tax evidence as its own immutable-source-keyed signed event/revision with source API version, report ID, event ID, effective tax date, observed/published time, raw-field name, amount in RUB, and classification. Re-ingestion upserts the same source identity, never doubles it. Never derive gross as Finance net payout plus all fee buckets: credits, subsidies, corrections and timing invalidate that shortcut. Until validated, UI state is `SOURCE_UNVERIFIED`, not zero tax.

## 4. Expense mapping, separate from V4

`AUTO_YES` means law/category and individual payment/document evidence are machine-verifiable; no present WB field alone is sufficient for that unqualified state. For USN income-only, all expense rows have `TAX_EFFECT=0` regardless of business P&L. For income-minus-expenses, start with these candidate rules; the shown status is the **current automatic status**, and a rule may become `AUTO_YES` only after Sprint 2 evidence checks. Preserve original signs and source corrections, not `ABS()` of source facts.

- Commission (`wb_finance`, `COMMISSION`, `ppvz_sales_commission`): P&L informational component already inside settlement; tax candidate `REVIEW` pending WB report/withholding proof, then eligible at legal withholding/payment date. Return/corrected commission reverses same source identity. FNS expressly permits marketplace agent commission.
- Acquiring, `PPVZ_REWARD`, `PPVZ_VW` (`wb_finance`): P&L fee components; `REVIEW`, subject to source meaning, contractual charge, payment and document evidence. Do not also expense aggregate Marketplace Fee.
- Logistics and return logistics (`wb_finance`): V4 expense; `REVIEW`, generally business service if paid/documented. Reverse signed credits; return logistics may remain a separate service expense after a customer return.
- Storage (`wb_finance`): V4 expense; `REVIEW`, same proof rule.
- Acceptance (`wb_finance` suffix `acceptance`, P&L `OTHER`): V4 expense; `REVIEW`, classify tax independently from P&L `OTHER` and require report proof.
- WB ads (`wb_ads.spend`): V4 advertising; `REVIEW`, campaign spend does not prove invoiced/paid service; reconcile to WB advertising charge, exclude duplicate Finance deductions. Refund becomes signed reversal.
- Penalty (`wb_finance.PENALTY`): V4 expense; `REVIEW` by exact legal nature and contract. Tax/public fines are `AUTO_NO`; contractual damages can differ. Never blanket-allow WB penalty.
- Adjustment/deduction (`wb_finance.ADJUSTMENT`): V4 adjustment; `REVIEW`, inspect the operation label/document to separate cost, refunded cost, withheld tax, or correction.
- Compensation/reimbursement (`wb_finance.COMPENSATION`): V4 credit; `REVIEW` as potential taxable income or expense recovery, never default expense. Apply signed source-linked correction.
- `OTHER` and unmapped finance suffix: V4 classification unchanged; `REVIEW`, including `for_pay` which is settlement and must be `AUTO_NO` as a tax expense. Unknown does not mean deductible.
- Product Cost (`product_cost_history` / V4 COGS): business profit only; `AUTO_NO` **as a direct tax deduction source**. Paid purchase lots recognized upon qualifying resale become tax expense in a separate tax calculation, so no double-counting Product Cost and purchases.

Marketplace tax totals come from Finance **component source keys** and ads source keys with a registry of tax rule/version, proof and reversal. Do not materialize a second independent mutable expense ledger; use a derived auditable tax view/calculation over source facts and documented overrides. A source-identity exclusion constraint prevents a WB ad charge represented in both ads and Finance from being deducted twice. Any missing publication, payment or direction evidence is `REVIEW` and is excluded from a confirmed subtotal, with a visible unresolved amount.

## 5. Purchases and simple operating-expense UX

Keep Purchases as a separate account-scoped workflow and preserve its Product Cost side effect exactly as is. Tax merchandise cost needs new *tax-only* evidence: paid purchase/credit amount and date, supported RUB conversion and VAT basis, sold/returned units linked to a specific purchase lot or an accountant-approved allocation method, and documentation reference. The five current invoice fields do not establish any of these. Never use `product_cost_history` times WB quantity as a deductible tax value. For an unpaid or unallocated purchase show `PENDING_TAX_EVIDENCE`; zero purchases is not evidence of zero cost. Company aggregation resolves purchase account → company; shared supplier articles across accounts must not share tax lots.

Expenses / Giderler presents Purchases (existing), Operating Expenses (manual), and Marketplace Expenses (read-only sourced amounts, eligible/review totals and category detail). Manual form has exactly Date, Category, Description, Amount and Tax Deductible checkbox. Category sets checkbox default and help text; user toggle sets `decision_source=USER_OVERRIDE`, preserves prior/default decision and editor/time. Backend manages creator, revision, source, currency and evidence status. A checked box is a **claim for review**, not by itself proof that payment/documentation conditions have been met. Without a verified payment/evidence path, checked manual expenses are displayed as `CLAIMED_PENDING_EVIDENCE` and excluded from a confirmed estimate; an explicitly labeled provisional scenario may include them. This resolves the conflict between the requested five-field UX and legal cash/document tests without silently treating expense date as payment date.

Category defaults (all can be toggled; a toggle never bypasses evidence review):

- Accounting, rent, software/SaaS, business advertising, business logistics, banking/payment services, professional services: `CONDITIONAL/REVIEW`, checkbox defaults **on** as a business-intent suggestion; paid, documented, business-purpose and category-specific rules are required.
- Payroll/salary related: `CONDITIONAL/REVIEW`, checkbox defaults **off** until payroll, employer/employee status, withholding and contribution treatment are specified. Insurance contributions are not a generic manual expense for income-only reduction.
- Office: `CONDITIONAL/REVIEW`, checkbox defaults **off** because mixed personal/business use and capital goods need classification.
- Tax/fees: `CONDITIONAL/REVIEW`, checkbox defaults **off**; USN tax itself, VAT, state duty and other taxes have different treatments.
- Fines/penalties: `DEFAULT_NON_DEDUCTIBLE`, checkbox defaults **off** for public fines; contractual penalties route to review.
- Groceries/personal: `DEFAULT_NON_DEDUCTIBLE`, checkbox defaults **off**; a business hospitality claim needs separate documented review, never automatic.
- Other: `CONDITIONAL/REVIEW`, checkbox defaults **off**.

These are UI defaults, **not automatic legal deductions**. [FNS expense criteria and 2026 expansion](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/html/sites/www.rn59.nalog.ru/Zakoni/202602izmZ.pdf), [FNS USN rules](https://www.nalog.gov.ru/rn77/taxation/taxes/usn/).

## 6. Minimal V1 data model proposal

`company_tax_profiles`: `id`, `company_id` FK, `tax_system=USN`, `tax_object` (`INCOME`/`INCOME_MINUS_EXPENSES`), `rate_percent`, `effective_from`, `effective_to` (half-open), `region_code` nullable, `rate_basis_reference` nullable, `vat_status` (`UNKNOWN`/`EXEMPT`/`VAT_APPLICABLE`, informational until validated), `approved_by/at`, timestamps. Check 0–100 rates and dates; exclude overlapping effective intervals per company. Store `minimum_rate_percent=1` only for income-minus-expenses **as a versioned legal rule**, not an arbitrary editable field. Do not auto-create 6% profiles from legacy default percent. A profile change during a tax year requires an accounting review; UI should favor next-year effective start.

`company_expenses`: `id`, `company_id` FK, `expense_date`, `category`, `description`, `amount_rub` positive, `tax_deductible`, `decision_source` (`CATEGORY_DEFAULT`/`USER_OVERRIDE`), `default_tax_deductible`, `evidence_status` (`UNVERIFIED`/`VERIFIED`/`REJECTED`), `created_by`, `updated_by`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `deleted_at`. Server-maintained revision history stores old/new amount, date, category, tax claim and actor; soft delete is an auditable reversal. No generic `source` selector or payment field in the entry form. Only RUB in V1 manual entry unless an explicit FX evidence process is designed.

`tax_source_events` (needed when source proven): company/account FK, `event_kind` (`INCOME`, `REFUND`, `REIMBURSEMENT`, `MARKETPLACE_COST`, `COST_REVERSAL`), signed amount in kopeks/RUB numeric, tax-effective date, source system/type/key, WB `report_id/rrd_id` where present, field name, publication/observed timestamps, rule version, evidence status, original/reversal link, unique `(marketplace_account_id, source_type, source_key, event_kind)`; corrections version facts rather than destroy history. Do not duplicate whole WB payloads or secrets. A narrowly scoped `tax_expense_rules` versioned registry maps WB source suffix + operation label + legal treatment to eligible/review/excluded; unresolved labels fail closed.

`purchase_tax_allocations` is **deferred until purchase evidence is built**: purchase-line/lot FK, verified paid amount/date, allocated sale/return source identity and units, tax-effective date, signed RUB amount, allocation method/version and evidence reference; unique lot/sale allocation identity, sum of deductions cannot exceed paid eligible lot cost. This is a tax subledger, not a replacement for Product Cost history.

Use company-scoped authorization/RLS on all new tables; account foreign keys must be checked against the same company. `service_role` access stays server-side. Store calculation run metadata (company, as-of date, profile/rule version, source-watermarks, included/review totals and hash) for reproducible exports; persist full result rows only if required by audit/reproduction, never as an independent editable accounting balance. Add indexes for `(company_id, effective_from)`, `(company_id, expense_date)`, `(company_id, tax_effective_date)` and source uniqueness. Schema work is Sprint 2, after restored-DB rehearsal.

## 7. Calculation, dates and reversals

Tax year and source event dates use `Europe/Moscow` for WB and company-effective tax dates; preserve original UTC timestamps and never shift a civil date by browser timezone. For each company and as-of date, select the approved profile effective on each tax event date. If no profile, source validation, or required coverage, state is `UNAVAILABLE/INCOMPLETE`, not a numeric zero.

`I_YTD = sum(signed eligible gross income/refund/reimbursement tax events from Jan 1 to as-of)`.

For USN income: `gross_estimate_YTD = max(0, I_YTD) × rate`, subject to a separately designed insurance-contribution reduction; label it **before reductions** until that feature is implemented. No marketplace/purchase/manual expense reduces this base.

For USN income-minus-expenses: `E_YTD = paid/documented/recognized marketplace cost + sold-and-paid purchase cost + verified eligible manual cost`, each net of signed reversals. `base_YTD = max(0, I_YTD − E_YTD)`. `ordinary_YTD = base_YTD × rate`. `minimum_reference_YTD = max(0, I_YTD) × 1%`, labeled projection/reference only before year end. At finalized year end, `annual_tax = max(ordinary_annual, minimum_annual)` subject to statutory loss/carryforward adjustments outside initial scope. Never convert negative base into a current tax credit automatically.

`selected_period_impact` is the change in **cumulative** calculated estimate between period end and day before period start, with profile/rule and source revisions held consistently; it may be negative and is not an advance/payment due. An advance/payment view requires paid advances, prior-quarter calculations and contribution reductions, which V1 does not have. At quarter boundaries present the cumulative calculation and label payable as unavailable until those inputs are supplied. If a company changed regime midyear, block automatic YTD synthesis and require accountant-approved transition handling.

Income/refund/cost events are signed, keyed and dated independently. A return of a prior-year sale can reduce current-year income on its qualifying refund date; preserve link to original. WB corrections/reissued reports supersede the same source key with revision history and as-of recomputation. Purchase return/credit reverses eligible lot cost on its legal recognition date, restores/adjusts available units, and prevents a subsequent duplicate deduction. Manual edit/delete creates a versioned correction; no hard delete of an already reported tax fact. Never `ABS()` away direction at the tax boundary.

## 8. UI, reports and V4 integration

New-company onboarding requires Company Name and Tax Model; Custom asks both tax object and rate, plus a short confirmation that regional/special eligibility is the user's responsibility. Existing companies show `Tax profile not configured` until explicitly configured; `default_tax_percent` is displayed only as legacy V4 setting during transition. Company profile is never copied to individual accounts. Marketplace expense screen shows business cost, eligible amount, excluded amount, review amount, rule version and source-watermark, with the clear warning that V4 Marketplace Fee is informational.

Tax dashboard: profile and effective dates; taxable gross income YTD; rate; source coverage/last publication; verified estimate. Income-minus-expenses adds marketplace, merchandise/purchases and manual eligible totals; unresolved totals; tax base; ordinary tax; 1% annual reference. Selected-period impact is separate. Warning precedence: `INCOMPLETE SALES` and `AWAITING FINANCE PUBLICATION` block tax completeness; `REVIEW EXPENSES` blocks a confirmed income-minus-expenses deduction total; `MISSING PRODUCT COST` warns V4 P&L only unless the separate purchase-tax allocations are also incomplete. An income-only tax estimate may proceed without cost data, but still needs gross income proof and any relevant reductions. Missing data never appears as a confirmed zero.

Later exports: Tax Summary, Manual Expenses with override/revision provenance, Marketplace Tax Expenses with source keys/rules, Deductible Breakdown, Tax Base and Estimated Tax. Include company, tax object/rate/effective interval, timezone, as-of/coverage dates, rule/source version, confirmed versus review totals and caveats in CSV/XLSX/PDF. Preserve signed corrections and spreadsheet-formula escaping. No live WB calls in reads or exports.

Target separation: Financial Engine V4 remains the immutable commercial calculation during Sprint 2. Tax Engine V1 runs beside it and provides a separately labeled company-level tax estimate. Historical V4 Final Net Profit and Smart Pricing remain on their existing formulas, with explicit labels. A future version may use approved Tax Engine output in a new `after_tax_profit_v2` while keeping V4 historical metric/version intact; replacing V4 Final Net Profit or retroactively restating exports is **Human Gate 3**. Do not write Tax Engine values into `wb_finance`, `wb_sales`, or Product Cost history.

## 9. Company and tenant boundaries

Resolve `company_id → marketplace_accounts → source facts` server-side; aggregate each account once; include inactive accounts for their historical effective periods. Reject any source/purchase row whose account does not belong to the selected company, even under service-role queries. Company manual expenses enter once at company level; purchase lines remain account-scoped and are joined through account ownership; WB fact IDs are unique within account, not globally. Company membership and RLS must govern tax profile, expense, evidence, calculation and export reads/writes; no anonymous access. Account connected midyear requires a coverage-start warning and prior-period import or explicit partial-year label.

## 10. Sprint 2 sequence and acceptance

1. Obtain Human Gate 3 decisions below. Prove a sanitized source-contract matrix for gross receipts, payment/refund dates, WB discounts and VAT against WB reports and accountant documents. No tax number until approved.
2. Add versioned company profiles and company-scoped RLS; create onboarding/profile UI without changing V4 default-percent behavior. Migrate existing companies to `UNCONFIGURED`, not assumed 6% USN.
3. Add manual company expenses, default/override audit and three-section Expenses UI. Build marketplace source/rule read model with review totals; no deductions granted from P&L labels alone.
4. Add validated tax income events and payment/document evidence states. Add purchase tax allocations only after lot/payment/sale linking and FX/VAT policy is defined. Rehearse migrations and rollback/safe-hold on restored DB.

Acceptance cases: (a) 6% company calculates gross income before marketplace commission and labels contribution reduction absent; (b) 15% company deducts only verified recognized costs and shows annual 1% reference separately; (c) custom rate requires object and effective period; (d) two accounts in one company aggregate once while a second company remains isolated; (e) manual checked/unchecked and category default/override retain provenance and unverified checked amount is not confirmed; (f) commission/ads duplicated across feeds deduct once; (g) paid unsold, sold unpaid, partially paid and returned merchandise never over-deduct; (h) sale/return, prior-year refund, fee reversal and report correction retain sign/date/source; (i) negative base gives zero ordinary estimate, not a negative tax; (j) YTD versus selected-period delta and annual minimum differ correctly; (k) missing profile, Sales/Finance publication, payment, Product Cost and purchase evidence have distinct UI statuses; (l) historical V4 numbers/Smart Pricing unchanged; (m) authenticated tenant cannot read/write another company's facts, and export equals query totals; (n) resync is idempotent and a revised source row is auditable.

## 11. Human Gate 3 decisions before numeric launch

1. **Tax income source and recognition:** accountant/owner approves the exact WB gross field(s), customer-payment/agent-receipt timestamp, treatment of WB-funded discounts, delayed payments, partial returns, VAT exclusion and reconciliation tolerance. Current warehouse cannot prove these. Recommendation: published Finance V1 gross report facts **plus independently verified receipt evidence**, not existing `wb_sales.revenue` or Finance `for_pay` alone.
2. **Purchase tax method and evidence:** select lot allocation (e.g. FIFO or specifically identified), acceptable supplier-payment and resale proof, RUB/FX date and VAT treatment, partial payments and credits. No existing purchase table proves them. Until then merchandise deduction remains unavailable, not inferred from Product Cost.
3. **Profile/legal scope:** confirm each company's actual USN object, legal form, applicable regional rate/eligibility, VAT status, and whether income-only insurance reductions or loss carryforwards must be in V1. Current `default_tax_percent=6` is not proof.
4. **V4 integration:** approve any future change in Final Net Profit semantics, Smart Pricing tax assumptions, historical reports or exports. Sprint 2 shadow mode needs no such approval because V4 remains unchanged.

No production tax estimate may claim completeness until decisions 1–3 and evidence checks pass. The data model, fail-closed states and shadow-mode implementation can proceed meanwhile without inventing rules.
