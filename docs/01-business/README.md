## What belongs here

- Business model, accounting rules, KPI catalog, glossary
- Approved definitions when later sprints fill them

## Canonical vocabulary

[GLOSSARY.md](./GLOSSARY.md) is the authoritative terminology source (Production). Later business docs must follow it.

## Canonical accounting

[ACCOUNTING_RULES.md](./ACCOUNTING_RULES.md) is the authoritative accounting framework (Production). Every money calculation must conform to it.

## Canonical business model

[BUSINESS_MODEL.md](./BUSINESS_MODEL.md) is the authoritative business-domain description (Production). Capabilities should support the domains and decision path described there.

## What does not belong here

- Implementation details (architecture / modules / widgets)
- Unverified invented rules
- Parallel synonym systems that contradict the glossary
- Formulas or interface field mappings (those belong in later technical docs, after these rules)
